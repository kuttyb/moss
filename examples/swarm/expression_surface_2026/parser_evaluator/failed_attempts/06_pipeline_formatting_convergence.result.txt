Observed in the complete application after its first `moss fmt --json` run:

  `moss fmt --check --json` returned FORMAT_CHECK_FAILED and reported that
  src/main.moss would change again. The first formatter pass changed the pipeline
  from uniformly indented stages to a partially stair-stepped form. A later formatter
  pass produced the fully stair-stepped form now accepted by `moss fmt --check`.

This is a formatter convergence observation, not an application-source workaround.
The representative source above has the natural uniformly indented pipeline layout.
